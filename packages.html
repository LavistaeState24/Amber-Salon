Do I need to book in advance for bridal or party packages?
Yes. Bridal and major event bookings are best reserved well in advance so trials, timings, and look planning can be coordinated properly.
Can I customize a package?
Absolutely. The packages listed are starting points. You can add specific services, upgrade to premium products, or combine packages based on your needs and budget.
Is the membership discount applicable to packages?
As per salon policy, membership discounts apply to regular hair and skin services. They are not applicable on makeup-based packages (Bridal Glow, Party Glam, Event Ready).
What is the cancellation policy for package bookings?
The Rs. 5,000 deposit for makeup bookings is strictly non-refundable and non-transferable. For other packages, we appreciate at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling.
Do you offer trials for bridal makeup?
Yes, we highly recommend a trial session for bridal packages. It allows us to perfect the look, test product compatibility with your skin, and ensure you feel completely confident on your big day.
