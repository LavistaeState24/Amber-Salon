Gallery FAQ

Questions about our portfolio and photos.

Ask About a Look Call Now
Are the photos in the gallery of real clients?
Yes, absolutely. Every photo in our gallery represents the real work done by our talented team at Amber Salon. We take pride in showcasing authentic transformations and genuine client results.
Can I bring a photo from your gallery as a reference?
We highly encourage it! Bringing reference photos helps our stylists and makeup artists understand your exact preferences. We can then tailor that specific look to complement your hair type, skin tone, and face shape.
Do you have before-and-after photos available?
Yes, we frequently feature before-and-after transformations on our Instagram and Facebook pages. If you are looking for a specific type of transformation (like a hair color correction or a specific facial result), just message us and we can share more relevant examples directly.
Am I allowed to take photos or videos inside the salon?
To ensure a relaxing, private, and premium experience for all our clients, we generally do not allow casual photography inside the salon. However, we are always happy to take photos of your final look for you to keep!
How often do you update your gallery?
We update our website gallery seasonally with our favorite looks. For the most recent, day-to-day transformations, we recommend checking out our Instagram page, where we post new client work regularly.
