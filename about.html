How it began

A salon born from a love for detail and genuine care.

Amber Salon by Heer Raval began with a simple vision: to create a beauty space where premium service feels warm, personal, and effortlessly elegant. Every appointment is shaped by detail — from consultation and hygiene to the final finish — so clients leave feeling more confident than when they arrived.

Tucked inside Sanskrit Galleria on Subhash Chowk in Memnagar, the salon has grown into a trusted name for Gurukul and surrounding neighborhoods — earning a 9.1/10 bridal score from 17 verified clients, and building a community of regulars who come back for the experience as much as the results.

Our journey has been guided by consistency, client trust, and a commitment to modern beauty care that respects both comfort and quality — whether it’s bridal styling, hair transformations, skin rituals, or everyday grooming.

Who is the founder behind Amber Salon?
Amber Salon is founded by Heer Raval. Her vision was to create a premium, calming space where modern beauty expertise meets quiet luxury, ensuring every client feels seen, cared for, and confident.
What makes Amber Salon different from other salons?
We focus heavily on personalized consultations. Instead of a rushed, one-size-fits-all approach, we take the time to understand your hair type, skin concerns, and personal style to deliver a highly tailored, premium experience.
Do you maintain strict hygiene standards?
Absolutely. Hygiene is a top priority at Amber Salon. We use sanitized tools, maintain clean stations, and ensure careful preparation for every single appointment to guarantee your safety and comfort.
Is Amber Salon a unisex salon?
Yes, we are a premium unisex salon. We offer a full range of specialized services for both men and women, including hair styling, advanced skin care, grooming, and makeup.
What is your core beauty philosophy?
We believe beauty should enhance who you already are — not hide it. Our goal is to highlight your natural features using premium products and expert techniques, ensuring you leave feeling like the best version of yourself.
