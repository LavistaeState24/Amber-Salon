Our story

Built around refined beauty, trust, and thoughtful care.

Amber Salon by Heer began with a simple vision: to create a beauty space where premium service feels warm, personal, and effortlessly elegant. Every appointment is shaped by detail, from consultation and hygiene to final finish, so clients leave feeling more confident than when they arrived.

Our journey has been guided by consistency, client trust, and a commitment to modern beauty care that respects both comfort and quality. Whether it is bridal styling, hair services, skin rituals, or everyday glow maintenance, the focus stays on delivering a premium experience with genuine attention.
