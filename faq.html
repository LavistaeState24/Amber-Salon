Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our salon, services, and booking policies in Ahmedabad.

Everything You Need To Know

Got questions? We have answers.

Where is Amber Salon located in Ahmedabad?
We are located at A/114, 1st floor, Sanskrit Galleria, Subhash Chowk, Tarun Nagar Part 2, Memnagar, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380052.

How do I book an appointment at Amber Salon?
You can book by clicking any WhatsApp button on our website, or by calling us directly at +91 95125 17604. We recommend booking in advance for weekends and bridal services.

Do you provide bridal makeup at the wedding venue?
Yes, we provide on-location bridal makeup services. Please note that venue charges start from Rs. 3,000, and a separate personal room is required for the bride and our makeup team.

What is the price of bridal makeup packages?
Our bridal packages are customized based on your needs. They start from Rs. 14,999. A mandatory deposit of Rs. 5,000 is required at the time of booking to secure your date.

Do you offer bridal makeup trials?
Yes, we highly recommend a trial session. It allows us to perfect the look, test product compatibility with your skin, and ensure you feel completely confident on your big day.

What are your salon opening hours?
We are generally open from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. However, hours can vary during festive seasons or holidays. We recommend calling ahead to confirm availability.

Do you have a salon membership program?
Yes! Our members save up to 20% on regular hair and skin services. Please note that membership discounts are not applicable on makeup services or promotional offers.

Do you accept walk-in appointments?
While we do our best to accommodate walk-ins, we highly recommend booking in advance to ensure your preferred stylist and time slot are available, especially on weekends.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?
Please note that the advance amount (including the Rs. 5,000 makeup deposit) is strictly non-refundable, non-transportable, and non-adjustable. We appreciate at least 24 hours' notice for regular cancellations.

Do you offer keratin, botox, or hair smoothening?
Yes, we specialize in all major hair treatments including Keratin, Botox, Nano Plastia, and Permanent Smoothening. Pricing depends on your hair length.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?
Yes, we use premium, high-quality products. Please inform us about any allergies or skin sensitivities while booking so we can choose patch-friendly and hypoallergenic alternatives.

Do you offer nail extensions and gel polish?
Absolutely. We offer gel polish, temporary extensions, acrylic extensions, cat eye art, and detailed nail art for both hands and feet.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and major Credit/Debit cards. Please note that orders are processed only after payment is cleared.

Who is the founder of Amber Salon?
Amber Salon is founded by Heer Raval. Her vision was to create a premium, calming space where modern beauty expertise meets quiet luxury, ensuring every client feels seen and confident.

Is photography allowed inside the salon?
To ensure a relaxing, private, and premium experience for all our clients, casual photography and videography are not allowed inside the salon. However, we are always happy to take photos of your final look!
